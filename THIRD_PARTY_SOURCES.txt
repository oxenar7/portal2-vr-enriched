Vendored third-party sources
============================

DXVK (DXVK-GPLALL with Portal 2 VR Enriched hooks)
  Upstream: https://github.com/Digger1955/dxvk-gplall
  Base tag: DXVK-GPLALL-2.6.8-2 (6a0ea561f9add008899680e6c313aa21c151e03e)
  License: dxvk/LICENSE (zlib/libpng)

  DXVK 2.6.8 plus async pipeline compilation, the GPL/async state cache
  and low-latency frame pacing. Submodule content is vendored at the
  commits that tag pins, headers and C sources only:
    dxvk/include/vulkan    KhronosGroup/Vulkan-Headers 2fa203425eb4af9dfc6b03f97ef72b0b5bcb8350
    dxvk/include/spirv     KhronosGroup/SPIRV-Headers  8b246ff75c6615ba4532fe4fde20f1be090c3764
    dxvk/subprojects/libdisplay-info
                           doitsujin/libdisplay-info   275e6459c7ab1ddd4b125f28d0440716e4888078
  Files meson would generate come from the MSVC tree of
  https://github.com/Hochgeschwindigkeitsrennfahrer/black-mesa-vr for the
  same tag: dxvk/include/shaders, dxvk/include/buildenv.h,
  dxvk/subprojects/libdisplay-info/pnp-id-table.c, dxvk/lib32/vulkan-1.lib.

  This is an altered source snapshot, not an unmodified upstream release.
  The VR hooks follow the earlier fork (sd805/fholger dxvk_l4d2vr, msaa
  branch, 75167aa2) and Portal 2 VR Enriched's own changes. Portal 2 VR Enriched changes:
    dxvk/include/version.h          (v2.6.8-2+portal2vr)
    dxvk/src/d3d9/d3d9_common_texture.cpp
    dxvk/src/d3d9/d3d9_common_texture.h
    dxvk/src/d3d9/d3d9_device.cpp
    dxvk/src/d3d9/d3d9_device.h
    dxvk/src/d3d9/d3d9_interface.cpp
    dxvk/src/d3d9/d3d9_vr.cpp       (new)
    dxvk/src/d3d9/d3d9_vr.h         (new)
    dxvk/src/dxvk/dxvk_openvr.cpp
    dxvk/src/util/config/config.cpp (portal2.exe profile: GPL on, lifetime tracking off)

  License: thirdparty/minhook/LICENSE.txt (BSD-style, including HDE notices)
